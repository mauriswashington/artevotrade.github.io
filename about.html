About Artevotrade LLC

Who We Are

A Global Consultancy Built on Expertise

Artevotrade LLC is an international professional training consultancy and technology transfer firm headquartered in Albuquerque, New Mexico, USA. Since our founding, we have operated across four continents, partnering with organizations ranging from multinational corporations to government agencies, hospitality groups, educational institutions, and international development organizations.

Our multilingual team of specialists brings deep sector knowledge and genuine cultural fluency to every engagement. We design and deliver training programs, operational consulting solutions, and technology transfer initiatives in French, English, Arabic, and other languages — always adapted to the local context.

Founded in USA Operations Worldwide Multilingual Delivery

Our Global Footprint

Americas

Headquarters and operational base in Albuquerque, New Mexico, USA. Artevotrade LLC is incorporated under New Mexico law and maintains its principal administrative functions here, serving clients across North, Central, and South America.

Europe

Active training and consulting partnerships across Western Europe, with multilingual delivery capabilities in French, English, and other European languages. Engagements span corporate clients, hospitality groups, and public sector institutions.

Middle East & North Africa

Corporate, hospitality, and government sector engagements across the MENA region, with particular depth in the Gulf states and North Africa. Arabic-language delivery and culturally adapted program design are core capabilities here.

Sub-Saharan Africa

Capacity-building programs launched across West, Central, and East Africa in partnership with educational institutions, NGOs, and public sector organizations. Programs are designed for sustainability and internal knowledge transfer.

Our Journey

Early 2000s

Foundation

Artevotrade LLC established in Albuquerque, New Mexico. Initial focus on professional training and pedagogical engineering, serving clients in the United States and developing the firm's instructional design methodology.

Mid 2000s

European Expansion

First international partnerships established with training organizations in Western Europe. Multilingual delivery capacity developed in French and English. First cross-border training programs designed and delivered.

2010s

MENA Entry

Strategic engagement with hospitality and corporate sectors in the Middle East and North Africa. The food & beverage consulting practice formally established. Arabic-language program delivery capacity built through local expert partnerships.

2010s–2020s

Africa Operations

Capacity-building programs launched across Sub-Saharan Africa in partnership with educational institutions, international NGOs, and government training institutes. A new emphasis on train-the-trainer models for institutional sustainability.

2020s

Technology Transfer

AI-augmented solutions and digital transformation advisory practice introduced to complement existing training services. Artevotrade now supports organizations in integrating technology effectively into their operational and learning ecosystems.

Our Core Values

Operational Excellence

We hold ourselves to the highest professional standards in every engagement. Our methodologies are rigorous, our timelines respected, and our deliverables consistently exceed client expectations. Quality is not negotiable.

Knowledge Transfer

We believe in building permanent capabilities within organizations, not recurring dependency. Every program we design is built to transfer real, applicable knowledge that grows and compounds long after the engagement concludes.

Cultural Adaptation

Generic programs don't change organizations. We invest deeply in understanding the local culture, language, sector context, and organizational dynamics before designing anything. Cultural relevance is built into every deliverable from the first line.

Measurable Impact

Every engagement begins with defining success metrics. We use pre- and post-assessment frameworks, KPI dashboards, and post-delivery impact reports to ensure our clients can demonstrate the return on their investment in clear, quantifiable terms.

Why Organizations Choose Us

Proven Track Record

Over two decades of delivering complex, cross-cultural training and consulting engagements across four continents — with client relationships that span years, not projects.

Expert Practitioners

End-to-End Partnership
