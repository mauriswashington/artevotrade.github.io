Who We Are

A Global Consultancy Rooted in Operational Excellence

Artevotrade LLC is an international professional training consultancy and technology transfer firm headquartered in Albuquerque, New Mexico, USA. We partner with organizations across Europe, the Middle East, North Africa, Sub-Saharan Africa, and the Americas to design and deliver high-impact training programs, operational consulting, and technology integration strategies.

Our firm brings together decades of combined expertise across professional education, hospitality, food & beverage operations, digital transformation, and organizational development. We operate with a client-first philosophy: every engagement begins with a deep understanding of your context, your challenges, and your ambitions.

Whether we are designing a competency framework for a multinational corporation, auditing service standards for a luxury hotel group, or guiding a public agency through its digital transformation, our methodology remains consistent — rigorous analysis, co-designed solutions, and sustained knowledge transfer.
