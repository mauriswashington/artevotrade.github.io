Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2025  ·  Last Updated: January 1, 2025

Artevotrade LLC ("the Firm," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the personal data of all individuals who interact with our services and website. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, on what legal basis, and the rights available to you depending on your jurisdiction.

This policy applies to all data subjects whose personal information is processed by Artevotrade LLC, including website visitors, prospective clients, current clients, and service partners. We comply with applicable data protection legislation including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), Brazil's Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD), South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), and Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

2. Data We Collect

We may collect and process the following categories of personal data:

  • Identity data: Full name, job title, organization name;
  • Contact data: Email address, telephone number, postal address;
  • Communication data: Messages submitted via our contact form, email correspondence;
  • Contractual data: Service agreements, invoices, project scope documents;
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type and version, time zone, pages visited, cookies (see Cookie Policy);
  • Usage data: Information about how you interact with our website and services.

We do not collect special categories of data (sensitive personal data) as defined under GDPR Article 9 unless explicitly required for a specific engagement and with appropriate consent.

4. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law:

  • Client contractual data: 7 years from end of engagement (accounting and tax requirements);
  • Contact form submissions: 2 years from date of submission;
  • Website analytics data: 26 months (if collected);
  • Marketing opt-in records: Until consent is withdrawn.

5. Your Rights by Jurisdiction

European Union / EEA — GDPR Rights

If you are located in the EU/EEA, you have the right to: access your personal data; rectify inaccurate data; request erasure ("right to be forgotten"); restrict processing; data portability; object to processing; and withdraw consent at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority.

California, USA — CCPA/CPRA Rights

California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, to delete personal information, to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do not sell personal information), and to non-discrimination for exercising these rights.

Brazil — LGPD Rights

Brazilian data subjects have the right to access, correct, anonymize, block, delete, and port their personal data, as well as to receive information about third parties with whom their data has been shared.

South Africa — POPIA Rights

South African data subjects have the right to access, correction, and objection to processing of their personal information, and to complain to the Information Regulator.

Canada — PIPEDA Rights

Individuals in Canada have the right to access their personal information held by the Firm and to challenge its accuracy. Consent is the primary basis for collection, use, and disclosure of personal information.

6. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data. We may share data with trusted third-party service providers who process data on our behalf (e.g., cloud hosting providers, communication platforms) under strict data processing agreements. We may also disclose data where required by law or court order.

7. International Data Transfers

As an international firm, personal data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside the European Economic Area. Where applicable, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) adopted by the European Commission, or other appropriate safeguards, to ensure an adequate level of protection for such transfers.

8. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies on our website. For detailed information on the types of cookies we use, their purpose, and how to manage your preferences, please refer to our Cookie Policy.

9. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, destruction, or alteration. These measures include access controls, encryption where appropriate, and regular security reviews. In the event of a data breach that poses a risk to data subjects, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority and affected individuals in accordance with applicable law.

10. Contact / DPO

To exercise your privacy rights, submit a data subject access request, or raise a concern regarding our data practices, please contact us via the contact form on our website or at our registered address. Please mark your correspondence: Attention: Data Protection.
