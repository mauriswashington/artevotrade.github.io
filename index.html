About Artevotrade LLC

Artevotrade LLC is an international professional training consultancy and technology transfer firm headquartered in Albuquerque, New Mexico, USA. We partner with organizations across multiple continents to design and deliver high-impact training programs, operational consulting, and technology integration strategies.

Our firm combines deep sectoral knowledge with cultural agility, ensuring that every engagement is tailored to the specific operational context of our clients — from corporate multinationals to governmental agencies and NGOs.
