1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognize your device on subsequent visits and remember your preferences or actions. Cookies can be "session cookies" (deleted when you close your browser) or "persistent cookies" (remaining for a set duration).

3. GDPR Compliance — No Tracking Without Consent

In accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and ePrivacy Directive, we do not place any non-essential cookies on devices of visitors from the European Economic Area without first obtaining valid, informed consent. Consent is requested via our cookie banner and is granular — you may accept or decline specific categories. Withdrawal of consent is possible at any time by clearing your browser cookies and localStorage data.

4. How to Manage Your Cookie Preferences

You can control cookies through several methods:

  • Browser settings: Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies through their settings. Note that disabling essential cookies may impair website functionality.
  • Clearing localStorage: To withdraw your cookie consent preference, clear your browser's localStorage data for this domain.
  • Opt-out tools: For Google Analytics (if deployed), you may use the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
